        How it works

        • We read each store's public online price list, the same prices you see on its website. The time of the last read is shown at the top.
        • We match items by barcode first. Without a barcode, we match only the exact same name and size, marked “matched by name”. Only items sold at 2 or more stores are listed.
        • Different sizes are never ranked against each other. Use the unit price (per oz, per lb or per count) to compare sizes.
        • Sale prices are shown as the store lists them, with the regular price crossed out. “4 for $5” deals are compared per item.
        • A price that looks like a different pack (for example a whole case under the same barcode) is greyed out and left out of totals.
        • “My list” adds up each store's prices for the items it carries, and says how many items it's missing.
        • “Best sales” lists only real sales: a store's own sale price next to its own regular price (from its website or its weekly flyer), ranked by % off. Items under $1, likely typos and “regular” prices far above other stores are left out. “Lowest in town by far” is not a sale: the same barcode, 25%+ cheaper than the next store.
        • “Flyer specials” are each store's weekly flyer specials, with the dates they run. They are not in the totals.

        Prices come from each store's website; in-store prices may differ. Some stores need a minimum order for sale prices online. Always confirm with the store.
